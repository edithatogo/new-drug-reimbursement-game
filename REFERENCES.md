# References

## Foundational source

Pekarsky, B. A. K. (2015). *The New Drug Reimbursement Game: A
Regulator's Guide to Playing and Winning*. Springer International Publishing.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-08903-4

Implementation references include Chapters 6–10, particularly the definitions
of reimbursement, IPER, the health shadow price, net economic benefit in health
units, and the three strategic games. Equation identifiers are recorded in the
source notes beside the implementation.
