# Method decisions — TA1121 research pilot

The primary convention is NICE-style 3.5% discounting for health outcomes and
costs, with DHSC's 1.5% convention retained as a sensitivity. This is a
research-pilot decision recorded by the repository owner and remains subject to
the claim boundary in the health-economist approval receipt.

Scenario 4 is explicitly sensitivity-only. Its Appendix 5 identity checks are
implemented, but no programme-aligned evidence is available to approve its
`mu`, `phi`, annual programme effect, horizon, or joint uncertainty for
calibration.
