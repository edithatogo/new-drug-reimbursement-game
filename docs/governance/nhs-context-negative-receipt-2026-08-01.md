# NHS displacement-context negative receipt

**Receipt ID:** `nhs-context-negative-2026-08-01`
**Reviewed by:** `edithatogo` repository maintainer
**Review date:** 2026-08-01
**Purpose:** bounded research-only pilot evidence search

Targeted searches of official NHS/NICE sources found current TA1121 and
tafamidis formulary/commissioning context, but no source identifying all of:

- a local accountable payer and budget boundary;
- an actual displaced programme with stable identifier; and
- a baseline cost and unit for the displacement parameter `d`.

The strongest results were NICE TA1121 resource-impact material, Hampshire and
Isle of Wight formulary guidance, Kent and Medway formulary guidance, and
regional formulary records. These establish national or local implementation
context only. They do not establish a local displacement decision or baseline.

## Disposition

The NHS context gate remains **pending**. The national NICE context remains
candidate-only, and the application must keep `d` unresolved. This receipt is a
negative search result, not evidence that no local decision exists. A later
owner-supplied payer or commissioner record may replace it.
