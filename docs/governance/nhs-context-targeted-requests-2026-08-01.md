# Targeted NHS context requests (not sent)

These drafts are based on the triangulated evidence matrix and request only
non-patient-level information needed to identify the displacement opportunity
cost for the TA1121 methodological pilot.

## Royal Free National Amyloidosis Centre

The public implementation records identify Royal Free as a commissioned
specialist centre. Please confirm, for TA1121:

- the accountable commissioner and budget boundary;
- whether acoramidis replaced, supplemented, or is merely an additional option
  alongside tafamidis or best supportive care;
- the stable programme/service identifier;
- decision date and price year;
- baseline cost and unit used for the decision; and
- whether the response may be cited as a research-only owner receipt.

No patient-level data or confidential net price is requested.

## University Hospitals Birmingham / Midlands Amyloidosis Service

Please provide the equivalent non-patient-level context for the service
identified in the implementation records, including payer, budget boundary,
programme identity, intervention/comparator, displacement, baseline unit, and
decision date/price year.

If the service does not hold the commissioning decision, please identify the
accountable specialised commissioner or record that the information is not
held.

## Accountable specialised commissioner

Please provide or confirm the TA1121 commissioning decision record for one
bounded programme context, including:

1. commissioner and funding boundary;
2. service/provider and stable programme ID;
3. decision date and price year;
4. acoramidis comparator and treatment pathway;
5. actual displaced programme and mechanism;
6. baseline cost and unit; and
7. owner confirmation that the programme is the source for parameter `d`.

## Handling

Responses will be hash-bound, classified as factual/supporting/negative, and
reviewed by the relevant-subagent panel. A response that confirms only
specialist routing or formulary presence will not close the displacement gate.
