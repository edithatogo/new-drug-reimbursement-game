# NHS decision-context request draft (not sent)

**Purpose:** obtain one non-patient-level decision context for the TA1121
research-only methodological pilot.

**Suggested recipients:** NHS England specialised commissioning, the relevant
ICB medicines-optimisation team, or the responsible tertiary amyloidosis
service/formulary committee.

## Request

Please provide or confirm, for a single acoramidis (TA1121) decision:

1. accountable payer/commissioner and budget boundary;
2. service line and responsible provider;
3. decision date and price year;
4. intervention and comparator (including whether tafamidis or another option
   was displaced);
5. actual displaced programme and stable programme identifier;
6. baseline cost and unit used for the decision; and
7. an owner confirmation that the identified programme is the source of the
   displacement opportunity cost.

No patient-level information, confidential patient data, or undisclosed
commercial net price is requested. A redacted decision paper, minute, or
commissioning record is acceptable if the required fields and accountable
owner are visible.

## Acceptance and fallback

The response will be hash-bound and reviewed by the relevant-subagent panel.
If the information is not held, withheld, or cannot identify displacement and
baseline, the response will be retained as a negative receipt. The application
will retain the national NICE context as candidate-only and leave `d`
unresolved.

This draft is preparation only. It is not an issued FOI or NHS communication.
