# T14 evidence sourcing playbook

## Ranked sources

1. Applicable legislation, regulator/HTA authority rules, official submission manuals, and signed sponsor decisions.
2. Official regulator/HTA APIs, registers, templates, validation tools, and published decisions.
3. Government, NHS, ICO, standards-body, and recognised quality-system guidance.
4. Primary methodological research and institutional validation guidance.
5. Secondary regulatory summaries for discovery only.

## Autonomous acquisition

- Search only official jurisdiction domains first for current legislation,
  guidance, templates, schemas, fees, timelines, validation rules, and change logs.
- Retrieve versioned documents and API responses; record effective dates,
  supersession status, section locators, hashes, terms, and intended submission type.
- Build a requirements-to-claim-to-evidence traceability matrix and triangulate
  each interpretation against both the controlling rule and implementation guidance.
- Monitor official update feeds or registers and invalidate the matrix when a
  governing source changes.

## Options

1. **Recommended — jurisdiction-specific validation plan after intended use and sponsor are fixed.**
   Avoids applying an irrelevant framework and gives a defensible authority chain.
2. **Research-to-regulatory gap assessment only.** Safe autonomous precursor when sponsor or submission type is absent.
3. **Multi-jurisdiction comparison.** Useful for planning but cannot authorize a submission.
4. **Research-only fallback.** Preserve the existing prohibited-claims boundary.

## Contingencies and stops

- Inaccessible or superseded guidance: use the authority's archive/register and record version uncertainty.
- Conflicting rules: controlling legislation/regulator interpretation wins; escalate unresolved conflicts.
- Missing sponsor/intended use: complete only the gap assessment.
- Stop before paid portals, credentials, legal acceptance, regulator contact,
  filing, submission, publication, or any claim of compliance.

## Refresh

Refresh at every validation/submission freeze, upon jurisdiction or intended-use
change, and whenever legislation, guidance, templates, model/evidence versions,
claims, or the repository commit changes.
