# Product definition

The project delivers an auditable new-drug reimbursement game application.
Its reusable native game representation and solving are supplied by the separate
Paignia library at an immutable revision. This application owns its economic
model, game construction, valuation, evidence and consumer acceptance.

Users are health-economics researchers, ontology and simulation developers, and
reviewers who need explicit assumptions, provenance and reproducible results.

The project does not provide reimbursement recommendations, HTA submissions or
regulator-grade validation. Paignia, Voiage, Kairos, UOGTO and Reimbursement Atlas
retain their capability ownership boundaries. Generic provider code is not
incubated or copied here after the Paignia handoff.
