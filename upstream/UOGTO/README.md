# UOGTO upstream proposal

Propose the files in `ontology/` as a new applied reimbursement-game pack or as
an expansion of the existing health-economics/HTA pack. The upstream change
should add:

- reimbursement as adoption plus financing;
- IPER, health shadow price, displacement, reallocation, EVCI, and NEBhR;
- firm, institution, and capital-market roles;
- contracts and evidence-development rules;
- conformance examples for Games 1–3;
- SHACL constraints and competency questions;
- mappings to the execution-binding extension.

Do not merge domain terms into UOGTO core unless they are proven general.
